About Tally (free lesson booking & level quiz)

All "Book Free Lesson", "Ready to Start", and "Book Free Trial" buttons on the site link to Tally forms. Tally processes the data you submit through its own secure servers and has its own privacy policy. I receive only the information you choose to enter, which I use exclusively to respond to your request and organise lessons.

About the waiting list form (EmailJS)

The waiting list form on the dedicated page is built directly into the website and sends submissions via EmailJS. The data you enter (name, email, level, optional notes) is delivered securely to my inbox and is used only to contact you when a group spot opens. I do not share this data with third parties.

About Calendly (lesson booking)

The booking calendar on the Bookings page is powered by Calendly. When you book a 50-minute lesson, Calendly collects your name, email address, and chosen slot. Calendly has its own privacy policy. I receive a booking confirmation and use your details only to manage and conduct the lesson.

Student testimonials

Reviews shown on the Home and Reviews pages are taken from public platforms (such as Google or social media). Only the student's first name and the text of their review are published — no photos, contact details, or other personally identifiable information.

If you are a student and wish to have your testimonial removed from this website, contact me at any time at info@englishwithmarcello.com and I will remove it promptly.
