About Marcello

FROM CANADA TO ITALY

I'm Marcello — a native English speaker from Toronto, Canada, now living in Milan. I moved to Italy in 2022, drawn by my Italian roots and a desire to build something new. What started as a personal adventure became a teaching career built on one simple idea: the best way to learn a language is to actually use it.

I've been on both sides of the classroom. I learned Italian from scratch in Florence, and I know the frustration, the breakthroughs, and everything in between. That's why my lessons focus on speaking and listening first — with grammar and exam prep tailored to your needs.

Whether you're starting from zero or refining your skills, I'm here to make the journey less daunting — and a lot more effective.
