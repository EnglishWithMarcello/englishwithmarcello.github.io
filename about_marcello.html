Who I Am

FROM TORONTO
TO ITALY

My name is Marcello, and I am a native English speaker, born and raised in Toronto, Canada. In 2022 I decided to completely change my life and move to Italy.

My choice was influenced by my grandparents' origins, who were born in Italy and immigrated to Canada in the 1950s. Thanks to their experiences, I received a small piece of Italian culture, and when I reached a certain age, I realized it was time to deepen and cultivate my passion for Italy.

My Path

THE JOURNEY

01
University & First Steps
After attending Western University in London, Ontario for five years, I decided to take a different path — studying how to teach the English language. The desire to move to Italy was growing, and teaching my mother tongue felt like the perfect opportunity.
02
Florence & Italian Life
I arrived in Italy in September 2022. During my first year, I studied Italian at the Leonardo Da Vinci school in Florence, fully immersing myself in Italian culture. Challenging, yes — but after many ups and downs, I succeeded in reaching my language goals.
03
Milan & Teaching Career
In September 2023, I moved to Milan and began teaching at YES School, where I delivered many courses — including in public schools through institutional tenders. Since 2024, I offer private lessons independently, bringing everything I've learned directly to you.

My Approach

HOW I TEACH

I believe that language learning is most effective when it is actively used. For this reason, in my lessons I focus on both conversation and grammatical concepts.

Speaking and listening are fundamental skills for achieving fluency, so I encourage students to actively participate in discussions on various topics — from the simplest to the more complex.

If you need grammatical support, I will personalise the learning path based on your specific needs, addressing any doubts or difficulties together.

I offer courses covering grammar, pronunciation, and conversation, available in both individual and group classes.
